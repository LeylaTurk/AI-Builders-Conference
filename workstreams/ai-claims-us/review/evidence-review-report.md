The shared calculator was independently run on all six revised JSON records: five 1s and existential risk 2. All checks are resolved Y/N; no applicable NC is hidden. Existential P=2/3 gives level 3; W=2/2 gives 1; their mean is 2. There is no double penalty for the same certainty problem. These are coarse ordinal wording scores, not harm or likelihood estimates.

The jobs correction is material: absence of a calibrated numerical forecast did not, by itself, establish a P3 failure for this reasoned possibility. The existential claim differs because its consequent asserts inevitability. Conversely, neither claim receives a penalty merely for discussing an unobserved future. Five grounded scores are acceptable for the selected bounded claims; the package must not imply that they represent the prevalence of hype across all public discussion. Qualitative evidence limitations remain visible rather than becoming unsupported numerical gap ratings.
