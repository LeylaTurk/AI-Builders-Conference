# Package acceptance checklist

This is the coordinator's completion checklist, not evidence that checks have passed. Actual findings belong in the independent review report and final package-validation report.

## Content, for each of six topics

1. An inspected public occurrence supports the exact claim or a transparently narrowed paraphrase; no invented stronger wording. Scope, timeframe and modal force remain visible.
2. A–G website sections exist. The short answer is about 50–80 words; total website copy is about 400–650 words before references.
3. Mechanism explanations, material evidence and factual premises of actions have inline links. Sources are opened and inspected rather than cited from snippets.
4. Evidence table contains exact assertion, URL, publication/update date, data period, geography/system, evidence category, limitation, locator, and relationship to claim.
5. Observations, experiments, projections, expert judgments and hypothetical arguments are distinguished where present. Missing evidence categories need not be manufactured.
6. Three to five practical actions link to current, specific resources usable by US readers and describe reasonable limits.
7. Exact claim and five hype checklist answers are documented; each answer has support, and the shared calculation reproduces the proposed score. Evidence uncertainty is separate from hype and harm.
8. Public metadata accurately distinguishes independent AI review from human review. No unsupported attribution to Leyla.

## Cross-topic checks

- Water and energy share definitions without confusing AI with all data centers or electricity use with emissions.
- Jobs distinguishes tasks/exposure/adoption/employment/causation and avoids individual predictions.
- Creativity distinguishes training/output/style/licenses/economic effects and identifies procedural status and jurisdiction for legal sources.
- Privacy names products and distinguishes defaults/settings/training/retention/access/disclosure, with dated policy checks and geographically limited rights.
- Existential risk tests both certainty and sweeping dismissal; separates misuse/loss of control/catastrophe/extinction and does not convert expert probabilities into measured frequencies.
- All topics apply the same scoring standard; scores are not adjusted to produce a desired distribution.

## Design and implementation handoff

- Current homepage and story inspected at desktop and mobile, or exact limitation recorded.
- One recommended section name, introduction, placement, navigation and responsive layout.
- Claim-under-examination label and essential qualifications beside claim and score.
- Keyboard access, focus behavior, meaningful headings/links, readable contrast and text alongside peppers.
- Real related-story IDs/URLs are distinguished from hypothetical matching. Missing/deleted stories yield an honest empty state.
- Handoff preserves existing feed/rating behavior and does not treat article scores as claim scores.
- Self-contained Lovable prompt identifies exact attachment and supplies all six finalized topic texts, sources, scores and review dates through that attachment.
- No runtime generation or factual rewriting; no unnecessary account/publishing infrastructure.
- Acceptance checks cover all six topics, mobile behavior, citations and existing feed functionality.
- No live website changes, publication, external messages or GitHub writes performed by this workstream.
