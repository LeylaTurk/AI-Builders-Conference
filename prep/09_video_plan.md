## Style: a Vox-like explainer

- **On camera:** Leyla introduces the idea and makes the key point to camera.
- **Voice over screen recordings and collage:** headlines on torn paper, phrases circled and highlighted, the source text beside the headline, numbers zooming in.
- **Shared visual identity with the app:** the same seven highlight colours, icons and labels as the markup view, so a viewer who learns "🟧 Hyperbole" in a video recognises it in the app. Paper texture and highlighter marks can carry into the app's design.

## Ground rules

1. **Practise what you preach.** Run every script through the app's own checklist: no hyperbole, every claim sourced, limits stated. Link sources in the description.
2. **Examples:** prefer the open-licensed backlog and press releases. Brief headline shots of big outlets for commentary are common practice, but avoid long quotes, full articles and logos.
3. **Accessibility:** captions on every video, plus a transcript shown under the video in the app.
4. **Time:** video work stays **outside the 40 sprint hours**. A polished 60–90 second video can take a beginner 2–4 hours; video 0 about 6–10 hours.
5. **Starting point:** video 0 goes in `prep/starting-point-log.md`, since it's made before the sprint.
