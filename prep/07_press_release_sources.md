# Research: press-release and open sources for the live feed

**AI, Seriously? · preparation research · 25 September 2026 · first pass**

**Goal:** a connected, auto-updating feed that doesn't depend on big outlets' permission (see [06](06_source_research.md)). Company and university announcements are where much AI hype starts, and they're written to be spread.

**Method:** web searches only; feeds haven't been opened from this session. Every row needs **(1)** the feed opened, and **(2)** the site's terms checked for an AI or text-and-data-mining clause.

| Source | Feed (per search results) | Why include it | To check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **OpenAI news** | Official RSS: `openai.com/news/rss.xml` ([source](https://best-ai-blogs.hushflowai.com/best-ai-rss-feeds)) | Launch posts with benchmark and capability claims | Site terms on reuse and AI analysis |
| **Anthropic news** | **No official RSS**; only third-party mirrors ([Alan Turing Institute list](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/ai-rss-feeds)) | Same as above | Whether a mirror is acceptable, or add by hand via the backlog |
| **Google Research blog** | RSS: `research.google/blog/rss` | Research announcements | Terms |
| **Google DeepMind blog** | Blog at `deepmind.google/discover/blog`; RSS not confirmed | Major capability claims | Feed and terms |
| **EurekAlert!** (AAAS; university and institute press releases) | Topic RSS feeds (not confirmed for AI) | Press releases about AI studies, often with the study linked: ideal for Behind the Claim | Terms: AAAS runs it, but each release belongs to its institution |
| **The Conversation, AI topic** ✓ licence checked | Topic pages exist ([AI topic](https://theconversation.com/topics/artificial-intelligence-ai-90)); RSS offered, exact URL not confirmed | Openly licensed, academic authors | Feed URL; "no systematic republishing" rule means we **rate and link** by default, republishing full text only for selected pieces |
| **ProPublica, technology** | Site feeds exist; topic RSS not confirmed ([technology topic](https://www.propublica.org/topics/technology)) | CC-licensed investigations | Licence says **select stories individually, not automatically**, so rate and link from the feed; republish only hand-picked stories |
| **Aggregated AI-company feeds** | Community lists of 30+ company feeds ([Turing Institute](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/ai-rss-feeds), [Planet AI](https://planet-ai.net/ai-rss-feed.html)) | A quick way to find more official feeds | Use them to *find* official feeds, not as the source |

**Proposed shortlist for the two feed sources** (to confirm once checked): **EurekAlert AI press releases** and **The Conversation AI topic**, with **OpenAI news** as the backup. Anthropic and others come in by hand through the backlog.
