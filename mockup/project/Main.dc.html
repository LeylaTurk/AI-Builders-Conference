Reading the ratings

Story score: how well the article is sourced, supported and put in context. Higher is better.
Hype: how far the framing goes beyond the evidence. Fewer chillies is better. Low hype doesn’t mean low risk.
Highlights in the marked-up view
Hype: overstated claims, hyperbole, human–AI framing
Evidence & sourcing: unexplained numbers, PR language, missing context
Good practice: independent experts, linked studies, stated limits
